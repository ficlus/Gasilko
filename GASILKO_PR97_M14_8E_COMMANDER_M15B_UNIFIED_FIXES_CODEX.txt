## 12. Fix G3 — STOP vs in-flight GPS samples and stale remote visibility

### The safety contract is three distinct moments

- **Local STOP tap**: revoke location callbacks and cancel any future
  sampling/signing/sending immediately on device, clear local position
  and mark server withdrawal PENDING until acknowledged. On devices
  with an in-flight request, the network packet may already have left
  before STOP. Do not promise physical interception.
- **Authoritative STOP commit**: within a single DB transaction
  transition session from SHARING to terminal, clear the latest
  coordinate, invalidate binding/reader visibility as required,
  preserve original STOP operation identity and outcome receipt.
  After this COMMIT, no GPS sample may insert/update that session's
  latest position, no RENEW may extend it, and `positions` cannot
  return a cached coordinate from that session.
- **Remote viewer receipt**: remote Android/Web screens must revalidate
  authorization and session state, clear at next authorized refresh
  (and within documented online 15-second maximum if source supports),
  independently discard locally expired fixes. An offline remote
  screen cannot be promised immediate notice of STOP; time-bounded
  lease/expiry and cache clearing remain required.
